NOTES:
 - DHCP snooping and Dynamic ARP Inspection are configured throughout this switch at the port level (trust, rate-limiting, sticky learning), but the global "ip dhcp snooping" command and the per-VLAN "ip dhcp snooping vlan" / "ip arp inspection vlan" enablement statements are intentionally not present. This is left off deliberately due to a Packet Tracer simulation bug with these features, not an oversight.
